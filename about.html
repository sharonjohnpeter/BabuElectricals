About Bhabu Electricals

Established in 1990, Bhabu Electricals is one of Tamil Nadu's trusted electrical contracting and engineering companies with over three decades of expertise in delivering dependable electrical solutions.

We specialize in HT & LT electrical works, industrial and commercial installations, solar solutions, infrastructure projects, and complete turnkey electrical contracting services.
