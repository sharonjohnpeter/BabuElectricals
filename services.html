Frequently Asked Questions

What types of electrical projects do you handle?

We handle all types of electrical projects including industrial, commercial, residential, institutional, and infrastructure projects. Our expertise covers HT/LT installations, solar power systems, panel manufacturing, and complete turnkey solutions.

Are your engineers certified?

Yes, all our engineers and technicians are certified professionals with extensive experience in electrical contracting. We ensure continuous training and adherence to safety standards.

Do you provide maintenance services after installation?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive annual maintenance contracts (AMC) and 24/7 emergency support for all our installations. Preventive maintenance is scheduled regularly.

Our standard response time for service requests is within 24 hours. For emergency situations, we provide immediate support with our dedicated team.
